For a long time, tracking employee attendance meant turnstiles, fingerprint devices or a paper
logbook. QRLog moves this process to the phone: the employee scans the QR code at the workplace,
and the system records check-in and check-out automatically.

## Why a QR code?

A QR code is cheap, simple and reliable. One poster on the wall is enough — every employee scans it
with their own phone. There is no need to buy a separate device.

## Preventing fraud

QRLog protects attendance on three levels: GPS confirms that the employee is at the workplace, a
photo is taken at check-in, and every employee is bound to their own device. This makes it harder
to check in on someone else's behalf.
