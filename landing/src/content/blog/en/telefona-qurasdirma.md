## Before you start

You will need:

- An Android phone and an internet connection
- The sign-in details from your manager

## 1. Download from Google Play

Open Google Play, search for **QRLog — Davamiyyət Sistemi** and tap **Install**. When the download finishes, open the app.

## 2. Sign in

Sign in with the details your manager gave you. If you have not received them, ask your manager or your company's administrator.

## 3. Grant the permissions

The app may ask for three permissions:

- **Camera** — to scan the QR code and take the check-in photo
- **Location** — to confirm that you are at your workplace
- **Notifications** — a reminder so you do not forget to check out

Without the camera and location permissions the check-in checks cannot be completed, so make sure you grant them.

## 4. Make your first scan

Go to the QR poster at your workplace, tap the scan button in the app and point the camera at the code. After a few seconds you will see the check-in confirmation on the screen. At the end of the day, scan to check out in the same way.

## Frequently asked questions

**The location looks wrong, what should I do?** Make sure GPS is turned on, move from inside the building to an open area, wait a few seconds and try again.

**There is no internet, can I still scan?** Yes, QRLog has an offline mode. The record is sent to the system when the connection is restored.
